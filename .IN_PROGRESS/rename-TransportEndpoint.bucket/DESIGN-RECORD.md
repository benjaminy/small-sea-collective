This document should remain blank / just this template until near the end of a branch lifecycle.
That's when we record the decisions we want to remember.

This document should contain a clear record of design decisions that could be useful to review later.
A human will move it to the Archive before merging the branch upstream.
It sits in a subtle middle ground between:
- Important project level decisions that belong in a proper architecture or design doc.
   These should be left out of the design record to avoid redundancy.
- Small facts about the implementation that can be re-derived by reading the code.
   These should be left out of the design record to avoid bloat.

For some branches there will be nothing in this middle ground.
Which means that the design record doc should not exist.
